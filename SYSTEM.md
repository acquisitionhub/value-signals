# How to follow value signals

One rule above all: **work down the action list from #1, and stop when you reach WATCH.**
Everything below WATCH needs no decision from you.

## The 8 tiers (strongest and most urgent first)

| # | Tier | What put it there | What you do | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🚨 EXIT NOW | A stock you hold turned red: target hit, thesis broken, stop-loss, price breakdown or a strong warning filing | Sell / close the position. The paper account does this itself | All of it |
| 2 | 🔻 BEARISH: TRIGGER | 3+ warning signs in the accounts **and** a warning filing (late annual report, auditor quit, restatement) | Read the linked filing. If it confirms the problem, take the capped-risk put idea | Max loss 1.5% of the account |
| 3 | 🟢 BUY: STRONGEST | Green ★★: fundamentals pass, daily chart 3+/5, weekly chart agrees, insiders or an activist buying | Read the thesis. Buy, or the long-dated call idea | 7.5% (shares) or 1.5% max loss (options) |
| 4 | 🟢 BUY | Green or green ★ | Buy a smaller position | 2.5%, or 5% with ★ |
| 5 | 🔻 BEARISH: SETUP | 3+ warning signs + falling weekly chart, put idea priced | Small put spread only, or wait for a warning filing to move it up to tier 2 | Half the usual: max loss ~0.75% |
| 6 | 👀 WATCH | Something is building: a warning filing on its own, smart money on an amber stock, bad accounts with a rising chart, a held stock losing its trend | Nothing. Read it once so you recognise it when it moves up | — |
| 7 | 🔵 HOLD | Held, no sell rule triggered | Nothing | — |
| 8 | ⚪ IGNORE | Everything else | Nothing | — |

Inside each tier, the stock with the most evidence is first: more stars, a stronger warning
filing, more warning signs, smart money, a better reward-to-risk on the options idea.

## Why this order

1. **Protect what you have before looking for more.** One unmanaged loss wipes out several wins.
2. **A warning filing beats a chart.** It's a fact on record, and it's what ends frauds (Wirecard).
3. **Agreement beats any single signal.** ★★ means four independent sources point the same way.
4. **Bearish trades come second to buys of the same strength.** Puts lose the premium if the move is
   late, so they only reach the top with a filing behind them.

## Your routine

**Every day (2 minutes, when the phone alert arrives):** alerts only come when something changes.
Open the dashboard and look only at tiers 1–2. Anything in tier 1 gets done today.

**Saturday (20–30 minutes, after the weekly report):**
1. Tier 1: confirm the exits happened.
2. Tier 2: open every linked filing and read the first page.
3. Tiers 3–4: read the thesis for each new green. Skip any you can't explain in one sentence.
4. Tier 5: note them. Act only if you have a small, fixed-loss idea.
5. Tier 6: skim. These are next week's candidates.

**First weekend of the month:** read the go-live scorecard. Are the greens beating the S&P 500?
Did the bearish ideas pay? Change one rule at a time, never several at once.

## Rules that never change

- Only capped-risk options: the most you can lose is known before you enter.
- No more than 15 positions; no single idea risks more than its size above.
- 3 months of paper trading first. Go live only if the scorecard beats the S&P 500.
- The system lines up evidence. You make the decision. Not investment advice.
